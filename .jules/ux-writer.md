## 2026-01-26 - Dropzone Instructions

**Copy Type:** Upload instructions (Empty state)

**Original:** "Arraste seu áudio aqui" / "Solte o arquivo aqui"

**Changed To:** "Arraste e solte seu arquivo de áudio" / "Solte para adicionar"

**Learning:** The original copy was robotic and slightly ambiguous ("seu áudio").
"Arraste e solte" is a standard pattern that is clearer than just "Arraste".
"Solte para adicionar" focuses on the action's result (adding the file) rather than just dropping the object.

**Rule:** Use "Arraste e solte" for drag interactions. Focus on the result ("adicionar", "upload") rather than the object ("o arquivo") in the drop state.
